# RoastLab AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

RoastLab AI is a playful comedy and roast-generation app. This policy explains how the current App Store build handles information.

## Data Collection

The current App Store build does not require an account and does not collect personal data on a RoastLab AI server. Mock AI generation is enabled by default, and app content is stored locally on your device.

## Optional Permissions

RoastLab AI may ask for optional permissions only when you use related features:

- Camera and photo library access, so you can select or capture photos for comedy prompts.
- Microphone access, so you can record voice prompts.
- Speech recognition access, so spoken prompts can be transcribed into editable text.
- Photo library add access, so you can save generated share cards or meme outputs.

You can decline these permissions and continue using other parts of the app. You can also manage permissions in iOS Settings.

## Local App Data

RoastLab AI may save preferences, generated roasts, transcripts, clapbacks, roast battles, personas, analytics-style usage counts, and subscription state locally on your device. You can delete local app data from inside the app where available, or by removing the app from your device.

## Photos and Face Data

RoastLab AI does not use face recognition, face detection, facial mapping, facial geometry, or biometric identification. When you select or capture a photo, the current App Store build processes the image locally on your device only to confirm the image can be opened and to read basic image dimensions and orientation for a generic comedy prompt.

RoastLab AI does not collect, create, store, transmit, share, sell, or retain face data, faceprints, face templates, facial landmarks, facial geometry, biometric identifiers, or any data derived from a person's face. Selected photos are not uploaded to a RoastLab AI server in the current build. Temporary photo data used for the on-screen preview and local prompt is replaced when you choose another image, cleared when the app session ends, or removed when you delete the app. Generated text saved locally can be deleted from inside the app where available, or by removing the app from your device.

## Subscriptions

RoastLab AI uses Apple's StoreKit for subscriptions. Subscription purchase and billing information is handled by Apple. RoastLab AI uses StoreKit subscription status only to unlock premium features in the app.

## Third-Party Services

The current build does not send roast prompts, photos, voice recordings, or transcripts to a RoastLab AI backend. If a future version enables a remote AI backend, this policy will be updated before that feature is used in production.

If you contact support through GitHub or another external service, that service may process the information you choose to provide under its own privacy terms.

## Children

RoastLab AI is intended for a general audience consistent with its App Store age rating. It is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Updates will be posted in this repository.
