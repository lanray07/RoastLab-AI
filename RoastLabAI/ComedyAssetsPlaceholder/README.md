# RoastLab AI Asset Library

The production app now uses SwiftUI-native premium artwork in `Components/VisualAssets.swift` instead of generic stock imagery.

Current systems:
- Recurring mascot cast: The Roast Professor, Meme Goblin, British Banter King, Savage Queen, and The Friendly Bully.
- Humanized illustration scenes: friends roasting each other, creators filming content, group chat banter, comedy battles, live audience reactions, and social media humor.
- Animated reaction avatars, comedy loading screens, achievement badges, voice-stage visuals, and share-card art.
- Premium themes: Comedy Club, Neon Roast, British Banter, and Meme Universe.

Future raster or 3D exports can live here when custom bitmap packs, App Store screenshots, or marketing assets are added.
